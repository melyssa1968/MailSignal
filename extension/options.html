Connect MailSignal to Gmail

Write and send in Gmail as usual. MailSignal adds a pixel only when tracking is enabled for an eligible email.

Gmail connection

After saving, refresh Gmail in this Chrome profile. Then check whether the extension loaded.

Connection recovery

If MailSignal is updated while Gmail is open, a notice tells you to refresh Gmail after your draft is saved. Your saved connection settings are preserved when you replace files in the same extension folder.

Sender-view protection

Version 0.5.0 blocks direct MailSignal image requests from Gmail in this Chrome profile. It also flags loads near messages displayed in Gmail as possible self-views. Google proxy fetches and other devices cannot be blocked reliably. Reload the extension and refresh Gmail after updating.

Latest send result on this browser

Refresh Gmail after saving. The extension is for desktop Chrome or Edge. MailSignal tracks image loads and HTTP/HTTPS links. Only recipient details, subject and link URLs are uploaded; the email body is not. CC/BCC are supported when at least one recipient is non-excluded. A load may come from any recipient, including internal copies. Excluded domains are managed in your dashboard. Live Gmail integration must be tested after installation.
